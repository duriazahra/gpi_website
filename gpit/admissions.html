Eligibility Criteria & Guidelines

Review the essential academic prerequisites before proceeding to the online application form.

Basic Academic Prerequisites

Candidates applying for the 6-Months Diploma of Associate Engineer (DAE) must fulfill the following mandatory institutional criteria:

Matriculation (Science Group)
Passed SSC with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with minimum 45% aggregate marks (or equivalent O-Level certification with IBCC equivalence).
Age Criteria
Candidates must be between 14 and 35 years of age on the closing date of application submission. Age relaxation rules apply as per Government regulations.
Valid Domicile & Identity
Candidate must possess a valid CNIC (or NADRA Computerized B-Form) and provincial domicile certificate.
Program Selection
Applicants can choose from Computer Information Technology (CIT), Civil Technology, and Electrical Technology, subject to eligibility and available seats.
Notice for Prospective Students: Official admission requirements, eligibility criteria, quotas, and deadlines are subject to the latest notification issued by the relevant authorities. Please check the official admission notice for updated information.
